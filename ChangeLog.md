# CHANGELOG MODULE TROMBINOSCOPE FOR [DOLIBARR ERP CRM](https://www.dolibarr.org)

## 1.0

Initial version, extracted from the trombinoscope of the Intranet module.
- Users or members as source, with filters by user group or member type.
- Member or user photo, whichever exists, served by Dolibarr viewimage.php (thumbnails + browser cache).
- Configurable fields, including complementary attributes of members and users.
- Newcomers highlighting, instant search, "Newcomers" widget.
- Automatic integration into the Intranet module menu.
- On first activation, imports the trombinoscope settings of the Intranet module.
- French, English, German, Italian and Spanish translations.
